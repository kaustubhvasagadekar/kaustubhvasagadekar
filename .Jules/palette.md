## 2025-05-14 - [BOGO-UI Accessibility and Contrast]
**Learning:** Product dropdowns (size/color) in selection cards require explicit 'aria-label' attributes when they are visually grouped by headers that are not programmatically linked via standard label tags. Additionally, the primary brand color must meet WCAG AA contrast ratios (4.5:1) for accessible text and interactive elements.
**Action:** Always verify contrast ratios for brand colors and ensure nested interactive elements have descriptive aria-labels.
