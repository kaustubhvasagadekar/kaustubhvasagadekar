## 2024-05-24 - Grammar and Contrast Polish
**Learning:** Micro-UX improvements like correcting pluralization ("Unit" vs "Units") significantly impact the perceived quality and professionalism of a UI. Additionally, using solid high-contrast colors (e.g., #757575) for secondary text instead of low-opacity black ensures WCAG AA compliance and better readability across different backgrounds.
**Action:** Always check for pluralization consistency in dynamically or statically rendered unit labels and prioritize solid hex codes for accessible contrast over opacity-based styling.
