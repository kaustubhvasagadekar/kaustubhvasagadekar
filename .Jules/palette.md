## 2024-05-22 - Brand Color Contrast and Semantic Grouping
**Learning:** The brand color `#FF6B82` fails WCAG AA contrast requirements (3.25:1) for small text on white. Using `#C2185B` provides a compliant 4.5:1 ratio. Additionally, grouping related options in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` is essential for providing context to screen reader users in choice-heavy interfaces.
**Action:** Always verify contrast ratios for brand colors on white backgrounds and use semantic `<fieldset>`/`<legend>` for radio button groups.
